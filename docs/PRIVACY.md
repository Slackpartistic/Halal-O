# Halal-O privacy policy

_Last updated: 1 November 2026_

Halal-O is a free app that checks whether a food product's ingredients are Halal, Haram or Mushbooh. It is built so that we learn as little about you as possible.

## What we collect

**Nothing is sent to us automatically.** Halal-O has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting.

## What stays on your phone

- Your scan history (the last 100 products, with their verdicts).
- Product details downloaded from Open Food Facts, kept for up to 30 days so the app works offline.
- Whether you have read the welcome screen.

Deleting the app deletes all of this.

## What leaves your phone

- **Product lookups.** When you scan or type a barcode, the app asks Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) for that product. Open Food Facts receives the barcode, your IP address and the app's name and version, as with any web request. Their privacy policy: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy
- **Reports, only when you send one.** If you choose "Send by email", your mail app sends the report from your own address to halalo.support@gmail.com. If you choose "Report on GitHub", the report is posted publicly on GitHub under your GitHub account. We use reports only to fix verdicts, and we don't share email addresses.

## Camera

The camera is used only to read barcodes. Images are not stored or sent anywhere.

## Children

Halal-O doesn't knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
